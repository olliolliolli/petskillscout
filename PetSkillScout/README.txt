PetSkillScout 2.7.1 - WoW: Forever (Interface 16001)
Hunter companion atlas: teaching beasts for pet skills, rare tameable beasts,
your active pet's abilities and stable pets.
UI languages: English, Ukrainian, Polish, German, French, Italian, Spanish.

INSTALL
1. Unzip so this file exists:
   World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/PetSkillScout/PetSkillScout.toc
   (The beta used _classic_beta_. To keep your settings from the beta, copy
   the WTF folder from _classic_beta_ to _forever_ with the game closed.)
   (Replace the old PetSkillScout folder completely.)
2. /reload or restart the game.
3. Open with /pss, /petskills or the draggable launcher icon.

SLASH COMMANDS
/pss              open / close the window
/pss settings     open the settings page
/pss rares        open Rare Pets
/pss pet          open My Pet
/pss route        open the skill Route
/pss next         arrow to the next stop of the route
/pss ammo         ammo count + arrow to the nearest place to buy ammo
/pss collection   open the Collection (looks, achievements, ranking)
/pss quests       open the hunter Quests tab
/pss passport     open your pet's passport card
/pss share        put your pet card into the chat input (you send it)
/pss queue        join / leave the taming queue of the rare near you
/pss scan         refresh character, zone and coordinates
/pss petdebug     print raw pet spellbook / action bar API data
/pss stabledebug  print stable API status and stable window widgets
/pss stableslots  inspect stable slot buttons (hover a slot first)
/pss raretest     preview the rare alert pop-up
/pss guardtest    preview the dungeon pet warning
/pss mapdebug     (world map open) show what the rare map layer sees
/pss arrow        stop the navigation arrow
/pss tooltip      switch the unit tooltip lines on/off

FEATURES
- Beasts: all 653 tameable beasts of WoW Forever in one list. Search by name,
  zone or family; quick filters Fast / Rare / Teaches a skill / For my pet;
  family, skill and rank dropdowns; sort by distance (nearest first), level,
  attack speed or name.
  Details: 3D model, level, attack speed, zone, spawn points, the skills it
  teaches (rank dots, pet level, new or known for your pet). Buttons: arrow,
  map, 3D view, Track (pins stay on the world map and minimap).
- My Pet: pet card (3D model, happiness, loyalty, training points, best food,
  Feed button), abilities and ranks read from the pet spellbook / action bar with
  rank dots, the next rank and "Where to learn", free skill slots (estimate),
  stable pets.
- Rare Pets: 82 tameable rares (incl. Season of Discovery and new Forever rares),
  wanted / tamed marks, radar that announces a rare when it appears on a
  nameplate, target or mouseover.
- Rare pets on the world map: one icon per rare on its zone (purple = rare,
  gold = wanted/selected, grey = tamed). Click: spawn area (all spawn points +
  outline). Shift-click: waypoint. Right-click: hide the area.
- Rare alert pop-up at the bottom of the screen with a 3D portrait, family,
  level and zone. Click: spawn area on the map. Right-click: close. Drag: move.
  /pss raretest shows a preview. Both features can be switched off in Settings.
- 3D model preview for creatures and stable pets.
- Unit tooltips (hunters): tameable + family + attack speed, whether your level
  allows taming, rare, the skills it teaches with the pet level, and whether
  your active pet already knows it / can learn it, plus your last sighting.
- Navigation arrow (TomTom style) to the closest spawn point, with distance;
  arrow buttons in Beasts, Rare Pets, the rare pop-up and
  Ctrl-click on rare map icons. It only shows the direction.
- Minimap icons for nearby untamed rares, tracked beasts and the arrow target.
- Rare sighting log: when and where you last saw each rare and how often
  (Rare Pets tab, map tooltips, unit tooltips).
- Pet care: happiness, diet and best food in your bags (My Pet tab), a warning
  when the pet stops being happy, and a Feed button (you click it; it casts
  Feed Pet on the chosen food). Shift-drag moves the button.
- Four themes: Journal, WoW, Classic, Moonglade (same layout, different colours/textures).
- Minimap button and shared rare sightings with your guild / group.
- Pet in dungeons: warning when you jump down with your pet following and when
  your pet fights far away from you, with buttons "Pet: stay" and "Pet: to me"
  (both also set passive). Key bindings: Esc > Key Bindings > AddOns >
  PetSkillScout. The game only allows pet commands from your own click or key
  press, so the addon never commands the pet by itself.
- Skill route (Route tab): pick skill ranks ("Bite 6", "Dash 2") or fill the
  route from your pet (best ranks its family can learn at your level). The
  addon picks a teaching beast for each one (tameable at your level, for your
  faction, with known spawn points), orders the stops by distance and guides
  you with the arrow. A stop is ticked off when your pet or Beast Training
  knows the rank; the arrow then moves on to the next stop.
- Ammo: arrows / bullets left on the My Pet card, a message when ammo runs low
  (and a big warning at a quarter of that), a check when you enter a dungeon,
  and the nearest place to buy: ammo vendors you visited (shared with your guild
  / group) or the nearest friendly town.
- Rare respawn timers: the time a rare was killed (or found dead / tamed) and
  how long it took to appear again; the Rares tab shows when it should be back
  and a "Respawn" sort. Kills are shared with your guild / group. Taming a rare
  marks it as tamed automatically.
- Hunter network: one click on "Connect" joins a hidden channel (PSSNet1), so rare
  sightings, kills, ammo vendors, taming queues and rankings are shared with
  all hunters with the addon on the realm (not only guild / group). Nothing is
  written to visible chat; it can be switched off in Settings > Hunter network.
  Wanted rares seen by anyone on the realm are announced to you.
- Pet cards in chat: "Share" puts "[PetSkillScout: Wolfie] Wolf 60, Bite 8..."
  into your chat input. Hunters with the addon can click it and see the card
  with the 3D model, skills and history; others read the text.
- Pet passport: a card for screenshots (model, where and when the pet was
  tamed, days together, skills, your collection and score) with a Screenshot
  button.
- Taming queue: hunters waiting at the same rare spawn see who came first and
  whose turn it is ("Your turn" when the rare appears). Joins automatically near
  the spawn of a rare marked as wanted.
- Collection tab: all 144 pet looks of WoW Forever (every tameable beast grouped
  by appearance) with 3D models, collected / missing / rare looks, arrow to the
  nearest beast with that look; 20 achievements with points; guild and realm
  rankings of hunters with PetSkillScout.
- Realm showcase: each look in the Collection shows how many hunters on the
  realm have it; the people button lists them (online first) and a click asks
  for the card of their pet with that look (active or stable pet).
- Training points planner (My Pet > Training points planner): plan ranks with
  a click on the dots, see points and the 4 slots, presets Solo / Dungeons /
  PvP / As now, and send the ranks you still have to learn to the Route.
- Quests tab: quest chains with strong rewards for hunters (bows, guns,
  crossbows, quivers and ammo pouches from level 7 to 60, incl. Rhok'delar
  and the new Forever rewards), for your faction. Status from the game:
  done (quest completed or the reward is in your bags / bank), in your
  quest log, available now, in N levels, or "yours is better". The gain of
  the reward for your spec is shown in attack power. Reminder in chat when
  a useful one becomes available at your level (and at login); the eye
  button hides a quest and its reminders.
- Quest ratings for every quest of WoW Forever: experience per minute
  against a typical quest at your level, plus gear upgrades from the
  rewards. Purple = worth it, normal, grey = not worth the time. Shown in
  NPC dialogs (the "!" turns purple / grey), under the quest window, in the
  quest log and in the Quests tab (modes "In this zone" and "Quest log").
- Quest markers on the world map: "!" at quest givers with quests you can
  take now (purple = worth it, yellow = normal, grey optional); click for
  an arrow to the quest giver.
- Optional auto-accept (Settings > Quests, off by default): only quests
  worth doing, or all. Hold Shift at the NPC to skip. This is the only
  feature that acts for you, and only when you switch it on.
- Settings page (gear icon): language, theme, window size, remember position,
  map / minimap icons and icon size, rare radar, pop-up, sound, chat message,
  tooltip lines, pet care reminder and Feed button mode.

DATA AND LIMITATIONS
- Quest database: AllTheThings commit 31a8fe6 (2026-11-05). Pet teachers and
  beast positions from AllTheThings: commit d503738 (unchanged since).
- 653 tameable beasts, 548 of them with spawn points.
- Families use the client names (Scorpid, Tallstrider, Wind Serpent, Crocolisk);
  19 families incl. Fox and Core Hound, with shared and signature skills per family.
- Every rank of every pet skill has its required pet level and training points.
- New Forever family skills (Sonic Blast, Swipe, Web, Pinch, Dismember,
  Tendon Rip, Savage Rend, Dust Cloud, Mine!, Trickster's Dance, Lava Breath)
  have no known wild teaching beasts; the addon says so instead of showing an
  empty list.
- Teachers for Demoralizing Screech 1-4 and Lightning Breath 6 come from the
  AllTheThings Forever database. Lightning Breath 1 has no wild teacher there
  either, and that database lists only 3 ranks of Thunderstomp.
- Data comes from the Forever beta (build 1.60.1.704xx). Re-check after launch.
- 305 skill / rank records across 266 NPCs with 10,956 spawn points, imported
  from a community dataset (2026-10-06). Accuracy on the live realm is not
  independently verified. Coordinates that are unknown are never invented.
- Rank and pet level data: WCLBox Forever pet database and Forever guides.
- AllTheThings Forever database (MIT, PetDataATT.lua): 48 more teaching-beast
  records (mostly dungeon beasts) and positions of 196 tameable beasts on their
  own zone map, used where the addon had no spawn points (e.g. Zephras Isle).
  Checked on 2026-11-04: 162 beasts' spawn areas match an ATT position within
  3% of the map, 33 differ (ATT often has one point, e.g. a quest objective; to
  be checked in game), 103 beasts (mostly in dungeons and new Forever NPCs)
  have no known position yet.
- The rare radar sees only units the client shows (nameplates, target,
  mouseover). It cannot detect spawns out of view.
- "Distance" is a straight line, not a travel path. The skill route orders
  stops by straight-line distance between them.
- Respawn windows come only from what you and your guild observed; there is
  no built-in respawn data. A window is an upper bound: the rare may have
  appeared earlier than it was seen.
- Network data (sightings, queues, rankings, learned quests) comes from other
  players; learned quest givers count as confirmed only when two players agree.
  Other network data is not verified. Everyone in the queue must have PetSkillScout to appear in it.
- Pet looks: mmonster WoW Forever hunter pet data (Forever beta), 648 beasts in
  144 looks. A look is collected when a pet with it is your active pet, in your
  stable, or a rare you tamed.
- Town list for ammo: towns with a general goods vendor (positions from the
  mmonster Forever world map); exact ammo vendors are learned when you open
  their trade window.
- The addon does not automate taming, movement or any protected action.

2.7.1

2.7.0
- Unknown quests are learned in game (Settings > Quests > Learn unknown
  quests, on by default). When you open a quest at an NPC and the quest
  database does not know it, has no quest giver position for it or lists
  other rewards, the addon notes the quest, its title, the quest giver,
  the map position and the reward items. Rewards are also noted at turn-in.
- Shared over the hunter network: records go to all hunters with the
  addon on the realm, and new players ask for them after login. A quest
  giver position counts as confirmed when two players reported the same
  spot (or you saw it yourself); far-off reports do not move it.
- Gear tab: quest rewards use these records when the database has no
  quest giver: the map button works, the tooltip says how many players
  found it or that it is not confirmed yet.
- Confirmed records will be checked and added to the addon's own data at
  the next data updates. Only quest data is sent; nothing goes to visible
  chat.

2.0.0
- New: Full interface theme (Settings -> Interface, off by default). It
  rebuilds the game screen in the addon theme (Journal, WoW or Moonglade):
  * Unit frames: player, target, target of target and pet with flat bars,
    class / reaction colours, health in numbers and %, level and rare/elite
    marks; a PetSkillScout badge over tameable targets (family, skill it
    teaches, rare) and a happiness strip next to the pet frame.
  * Action bars: the bottom bars stacked in the centre, pet and stance bars
    above them, flat square buttons with cropped icons; gryphons, bar art,
    page arrows and the old XP bar are gone.
  * Chat panel bottom left with an info row (time, fps/ms, gold) and an
    info bar bottom right (ammo, free bag slots, durability, pet mood);
    micro menu and bags above it, tooltips anchored there, item tooltips
    get a border in the item quality colour.
  * Square minimap top right with the zone name, mouse wheel zoom, XP bar
    (with rested XP) and pet XP bar under it.
  * Flat panels for Blizzard windows, tooltips and chat, and the Noto Sans
    interface font.
  Every part has its own switch. Switching a part off (or picking the
  Classic theme) needs a reload; the addon asks with a one-click button.
  Everything is built out of combat; nothing protected is called in combat.
  Switches itself off with ElvUI, Tukui, GW2 UI and similar; the bars part
  is off with Bartender4 or Dominos.
