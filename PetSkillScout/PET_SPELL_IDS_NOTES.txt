PetSkillScout - pet spell ID mapping notes

Source references:
- CMaNGOS playerbots, playerbot/PlayerbotFactory.cpp (Classic pet spell ranks)
  https://github.com/cmangos/playerbots/blob/master/playerbot/PlayerbotFactory.cpp
- ForeverDB spell 1310612 (Trickster's Dance, actual pet spell)
  https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310612
- ForeverDB Hunter spellbook (additional Forever-specific pet abilities)
  https://wowfwiki.com/wiki/Hunter_spellbook

Explicit IDs included: Bite, Claw, Growl, Cower, Dash, Dive, Prowl,
Thunderstomp, classic Screech (shown as Demoralizing Screech),
Trickster's Dance. Other pet abilities, including new Forever family
abilities and passive skills, are read from the client's pet spellbook
and GetSpellInfo where available. Their unverified spell IDs are not
invented. The action bar alone does not expose passive spells.

WoW Forever in-game validation is still required.
