RevUI - a flat, dark theme for the WoW: Forever interface, by DarthRev1.

Unit frames (player, target, target of target, pet, party), action bars,
chat, minimap, nameplates, buffs and debuffs, bags, the game's windows and
tooltips, the damage meter and the loot window, in one consistent style.
Works for every class: combo points under the target frame, a druid's mana
bar in bear and cat form, stance and pet bars.

Settings: Game Menu > Options > AddOns > RevUI, or /revui.
Moving frames: /revui move.

Hunters: with PetSkillScout installed, RevUI also shows the tameable badge,
pet happiness and pet food in bags. PetSkillScout's own theme switches off
while RevUI is loaded.
